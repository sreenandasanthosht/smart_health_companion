{# ---- Self-care score, with an honest explanation ---- #}
Self-care score
{{ health_score }}out of 100
{{ health_band }}

This reflects how well you have kept up with your own health routine — logging readings, taking medicines and sleeping enough. It is not a medical assessment and it does not diagnose anything.

How is this worked out?
Everyone starts at 100. Points come off only for things we can measure from what you have logged:
  • Recent readings — up to 35 points if several of your last readings were outside the usual range, plus 8 if your average blood sugar has been high.
  • Medicines — up to 20 points, in proportion to doses not marked as taken over the last 14 days.
  • Sleep — 6 points if your average sleep over the last week is under target.
  • No readings at all — 20 points, because we cannot tell how you are doing.
Only a doctor can assess your health. If you feel unwell, contact your provider — whatever this number says.
