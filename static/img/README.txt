What works well:
  * A real clinical or care scene -- a nurse with an older patient, a doctor
    in consultation, hands over a blood-pressure cuff.
  * Landscape, at least 1920x1200. The page crops to the centre-upper area.
  * Calm and warm rather than clinical-cold or stock-smiley.
  * The left half is covered by a dark green scrim for the text, so put the
    subject slightly RIGHT of centre.
