What CareNest does

Everything your health needs between visits

Nine parts of looking after yourself, in one place — built for people managing long-term conditions, and for the family and professionals helping them.

Track your readings

Log blood pressure, pulse, blood sugar and weight in seconds. CareNest shows you the trend rather than a wall of numbers, and tells you plainly when something has moved.

Vitals & trends

Never miss a dose

Set the times you take each medicine and get a reminder when one is due. Mark it off in one tap, and see at a glance what you have already taken today.

Medicines & reminders

Photograph your prescription

Take a picture and CareNest reads the medicines, doses and timings from it, then asks you to confirm before anything is saved. No typing out long names.

AI-assisted reading

Understand your lab reports

Upload a report and see the values pulled out and placed alongside your history, so you can see what has changed since last time.

Lab results

Stay with your provider

Book appointments, send your recent readings ahead of a visit, and follow the goals your doctor sets for you between consultations.

Appointments & care plans

Let family help

Link a son, daughter or carer to your account so they can keep an eye on your medicines and appointments — only after you approve them.

Caregivers

Share on your terms

Give a provider access to exactly the records you choose, for as long as you choose. Every time someone opens your records, it is written down.

Consent & audit trail

A private place to think

Note how you are feeling, keep a mood record, or write something down and throw it away. This section is never shared with providers or caregivers.

Mental wellbeing — private

Build better days

Water, steps and sleep, tracked against simple daily goals — the everyday habits that make the clinical numbers easier to manage.

Daily habits

Care doesn't stop when you leave the clinic

A consultation lasts fifteen minutes. The months around it are where a condition is actually managed — and that is the part CareNest is built for.

1

Before your visit

Arrive with your readings already recorded and your medicines up to date. Send a summary ahead so your provider has read it before you sit down.

2

During your visit

Your provider sees the history you agreed to share — not a blank page and a half-remembered account of the last three months.

3

After your visit

New prescriptions, new targets and new reminders carry straight into your day, with your caregivers kept in the loop.
